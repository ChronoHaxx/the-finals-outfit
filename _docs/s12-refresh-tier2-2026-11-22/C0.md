## TallSneakers (C0b): stopped

The coordinator's gate was all three of these:
- (a) no material reads the tile index;
- (b) none needs UV1;
- (c) the 17 Nov UV0 re-wrapped to the live tile layout equals the live UV0 within 1e-6, with a whole-number offset for
  every vertex.

All three fail on the Light run's GLB (`light-bulk-v2/source-glb/SK_Casual_TallSneakers_M.glb`, `cff3a74c…`):
- (a) `MI_Casual_TallSneakers_Alliance` reads `floor(uv0.x)`;
- (b) one material requires UV1;
- (c) positions are equal and the offsets are whole tiles (−2..2, V 0), but 970 of 4,454 UV components differ by more
  than 1e-6, up to 2⁻¹⁰ (half-float steps).

So TallSneakers is not in C0 and stays on its live Medium for now.

That live Medium is itself defective: its UV0 is wrapped and it has no UV1, so the Alliance print's `floor(uv0.x)` is
always 0. The 17 Nov mesh is the faithful one (raw tiles, UV1). The coordinator's note of 25 Nov records the follow-up
as a separate correction after C0. It swaps in the 17 Nov mesh only behind these gates:
- every sampler's wrap mode listed, with no clamp on UV0 outside 0–1;
- UV1 read where Alliance needs it;
- before/after full-size captures and ΔE for every TallSneakers variant, with no variant regressing.
