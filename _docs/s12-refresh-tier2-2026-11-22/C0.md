## TallSneakers (C0b)

The Light session measured the 17 Nov TallSneakers Medium against the live one: same geometry, and UV0 differs only by
whole tiles. The live copy comes from an older converter that wrapped U into 0–1 and dropped UV1. The 6 Nov read was
identical to live, wrapped UVs included, so the difference is the converter, not the game.

Proof on the Light run's GLB (`light-bulk-v2/source-glb/SK_Casual_TallSneakers_M.glb`, `cff3a74c…`):
- morph-only: refused only for the added `TEXCOORD_1` and `TEXCOORD_0` (positions, normals, joints, weights and morphs
  equal);
- surface: "reworked: UV sets differ, geometry within 1 mm". 2,227 vertices and 3,570 triangles on both sides. UV0 fits
  modulo whole tiles for 96%; the rest sits at the half-float step (2⁻¹⁰).

What changes on screen:
- `MI_Casual_TallSneakers_Alliance` reads `floor(uv0.x)` (the tile index). On the wrapped live mesh that index is always
  0, so the Alliance print probably routes wrong today. The raw-tile mesh gives the game's tiles.
- One of the 10 materials requires UV1. With no UV1 on the mesh, the app reads UV0 in its place
  (`ReconstructedMaterial.ts:699`); the new GLB carries the game's UV1.

So the swap should be more faithful. Review Alliance and the UV1 material against their icons before admitting it.
