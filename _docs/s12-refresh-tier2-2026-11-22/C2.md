The 43 `starterset-shirt-flag-*` items show `SK_Shirt_M` plus `SK_Samurai_WornCape_M`: the flag, worn as a cape. The
cape is wrap-deformed by its ClothSim proxy.
- The cohort's guard, inherited from w152, skipped all 43: they have no w80/w117 wrap evidence under their own ids.
- They are supported today, through the flag-shirts wave (`reconstructed-flag-shirts-v1`).
- Without them, C2 would rebuild 57 of the 100 materials, yet the Shirt swap would still reach the flag shirts.

The coordinator approved a narrow change, in the C2 cohort script only. A proxy-wrapped part without evidence also
passes when both hold:
- (a) the item is already in the active supported-items;
- (b) the part's mesh is not one of the wave's meshes.

The wave rebinds only the Shirt slots. The cape's mesh, wrap and material stay as the flag-shirts wave admitted them.
- Every part kept this way is recorded on its row (`proxyWrapKept`) and in `proxyWrapKeptSupported`: 43 items, each
  the WornCape part.
- Must-fail self-checks run before any row:
  - a wrapped part on a wave mesh is refused;
  - an item not yet supported is refused.
- Each check was shown to fail with its condition removed (a scratch copy of the script stops with
  "proxy-wrap self-check failed" and writes nothing).
- Captures to come: at least 6 flag shirts, live vs candidate, front, side and back. They will show whether the
  Season 12 Shirt changes how the cape sits: new poke-through at the shoulders, collar or back stops the wave for
